# Decision Gate - 2026-08-17

## Decision

Replace the block plan for week 3 (remainder) through week 6 with a shin-splint
return-to-run ramp: no SOS days, no back-to-back running days at first, mileage
rebuilt in small steps, with a reassessment gate at week 6 for reintroducing
SOS work.

## Facts

- Shin splints surfaced 2026-08-12 mid-run (1.73 mi, stopped early); no running
  since. 2026-08-16 was a cross-train hike, not a run.
- Actual mileage has missed target three straight weeks: 27.74/30-34,
  14.71/26-27, 8.62/31, 0.00/36 (current week, so far).
- The original week 3 grid already had two SOS days (Tue 7mi speed, Thu 7mi
  threshold) and a 10mi run with a steady segment Sunday — all incompatible
  with an unresolved shin injury.
- Race date is fixed: 2026-12-06, 15 weeks out after this one.
- Runner's stated best-case scenario: a 4 mi pain(ish)-free test run Wednesday
  2026-08-19.

## Preference

Rebuild mileage back to what the block needs before reintroducing SOS work,
rather than resuming the original grid's numbers or workout placement.

## Risk

The original grid's rate of increase (+5mi, then +6mi weeks) plus immediate
SOS days is a plausible contributor to the injury; repeating that pattern on
return risks re-aggravation. Back-to-back running days early in a return from
shin splints is a common re-aggravation trigger specifically. Mitigation: no
running two days in a row through week 3's remainder, flat/small mileage
increases week over week, and an explicit reassessment gate before adding
intensity back.

## Adaptation

Weeks rewritten in `plans/2026-half-marathon/weeks/`:

- Week 3 remainder (2026-08-19 to 2026-08-23): 3 running days, ~13 mi. Wed 4mi
  test run, Thu off, Fri 4mi only if Wed was pain-free, Sun 5mi easy (replaces
  the original steady long run).
- Week 4 (2026-08-24): 4 running days, ~18 mi, all easy.
- Week 5 (2026-08-31): 5 running days, ~25 mi, all easy.
- Week 6 (2026-09-07): 6 running days, ~30 mi, all easy.

**SOS-reintroduction gate at week 6** (parallels the existing 58-60 mpw gate
in `docs/decision_formats.md`): reintroduce SOS days only if 6 days/wk feels
normal, no shin pain during or in the 48h after any run, and easy pace hasn't
required guarding or an altered gait. If any of those fail, hold another week
at flat volume before adding intensity.

Weeks 7 onward, and how this reconciles with the original block's peak-week
and taper arithmetic against the 12-06 race date, are deliberately left open
until the week 6 gate is reached.

## Final Call

Adopt the return-to-run ramp for weeks 3(remainder)-6. `logs/weekly/week_2026-08-17.md`
day plans updated to match. Revisit this record at the week 6 gate and record
the reintroduction decision (or the extension) as a follow-up.
