## Risk

A 15 mi run would be nearly double the longest run in three weeks, on a
four-week running average of roughly 20-24 mpw. Walking keeps aerobic fitness
and time on feet but loads the legs at about 1-1.5x bodyweight vs about 2-3x
for running, so run-specific tissue tolerance (shin history, see
`decisions/2026-08-17_shin_splint_return_to_run_ramp.md`) is the limiter, not
fitness. Load-spike references: Nielsen et al. 2014 (JOSPT), Gabbett 2016
(BJSM). Short reduced-training periods retain most aerobic fitness (Mujika &
Padilla 2000, Sports Med). 58-60 mpw gate not triggered.
